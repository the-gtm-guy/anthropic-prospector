# How Your Numbers Compare to Industry Benchmarks (interview prep only)

**Rule:** Benchmarks show what is *typical*. They are not your results, so none of these figures go on your resume or LinkedIn as yours. Use them in two ways: (1) as a talk track in interviews ("industry surveys put typical outbound SDRs around 12 to 15 held meetings a month; I booked 30 to 35"), and (2) as a plausibility check on your own claims.

Only the Bridge Group is a primary survey. The other figures come from vendor blogs that often repeat each other, so say "industry surveys" or "published benchmarks" and don't quote a specific blog as fact.

## Comparison table

| Metric | Typical | Your number | How to use it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SDRs at quota** | Only **60%** of SDRs hit quota in 2025, the lowest the Bridge Group has recorded ([Bridge Group 2025](https://www.bridgegroupinc.com/research/2025-sdr-models-metrics-report-the-bridge-group)) | **162%** (Nuve), **130%** (Intaker) | "Only about six in ten SDRs hit quota in the latest industry survey. I cleared it by 30 to 60%." |
| **New-SDR ramp time** | About **3 months** (Bridge Group 2025) | Cut ramp time **28%** at CROP | "Industry ramp is roughly three months. Shortening it is the lever I'd pull as a manager, and at CROP I cut it 28%." |
| **SDR tenure and turnover** | Average tenure about **1.9 years**. Median turnover **40%**, which includes 16 points of promotions (Bridge Group 2025) | n/a | Manager-level point: retention and promotion are part of the job, not just hitting numbers. |
| **Meetings booked per month** (outbound B2B SaaS) | About **12 to 15** typical, 18 to 20 good, 25+ top performers ([Prospeo](https://prospeo.io/s/sdr-performance-metrics), citing Operatix; [FullEnrich](https://www.fullenrich.com/content/sdr-metrics-faq) says 8 to 15 for many SMB and mid-market programs) | **30 to 35 booked** at Nuve (about 23 to 27 held) | "Published benchmarks put top performers at 25+ booked. I was running 30 to 35." |
| **Show rate** | About **80%** typical, 85% good, 90%+ top (Prospeo) | **76%** at Nuve | Slightly below typical. Own it. If asked, say what you'd do as a manager to raise it (same-day confirmations, reminders, shorter lead time). |
| **Held meeting to opportunity** | Varies a lot by definition. Cold-sourced meetings: typical **15 to 25%**, good 25 to 40%, elite 40%+ ([Funnelfeedr](https://funnelfeedr.com/blog/opportunity-rate)). Some guides cite about 50% for AE-accepted opportunities ([Prospeo](https://prospeo.io/s/meeting-to-opportunity-conversion-rate)) | Not tracked at Intaker (revenue-only quota) | Don't quote a stat you didn't track. Say how you'd define an accepted opportunity and measure it as a manager. |
| **Rank on the team** | n/a | Top 3 on a 14+ rep SDR team (Nuve) | Be ready to name who can confirm it: a leaderboard, your SDR manager, or your sales leader. |

## Where the benchmarks help and where they don't
- **They help most at Nuve.** Booking 30 to 35 demos a month and closing 4 to 5 deals puts you well above the typical ranges.
- **They don't help at Intaker.** About 23 opportunities a month at a small average deal size is fine but not a headline. Lead with the 130% and the Home Services build-out.
- **Definitions vary.** "Meeting," "opportunity," and "quota" mean different things at different companies. When you quote a benchmark in an interview, state your own definition too.

## Caveats on the sources
- The Bridge Group study is a survey of 351 B2B companies (78% North America, 83% B2B SaaS), and it skews toward companies with engaged sales-development leaders. Treat it as directional.
- Several vendor sites quote 45 to 60% *average* attainment, which is a different measure from the share of reps at quota. They are not directly comparable to the 60% figure.
- The Bridge Group's pages list the 2025 report's title two different ways. Confirm the exact title on their site before citing it in writing.
