## 3. The 8 interview questions I'd ask

**1. "Walk me through the people who reported to you, by name and role, in the last three years. Who set their quotas, and who signed their reviews?"** *(formal management)*
> **Coaching:** Don't dodge it. Say: "Formally, two procurement technicians at the State of California. In sales, I've been a player-coach: lead rep at Big Bus and team-lead duties at Nuve, where Branden was the manager of record." Then go straight to what he owned: 30/60/90 evaluations, 1:1s, group training and sourcing hires. Honesty here earns credibility for everything after it.

**2. "Tell me about a rep who was below plan and what you did, week by week. Where did they end up?"** *(coaching underperformers)*
> **Coaching:** Pick one real Nuve SDR (or a Big Bus rep he reassigned to a new territory). Structure it as diagnosis (activity vs conversion vs skill), the plan (call reviews, role-plays, a 30/60/90 checkpoint), the metric that moved, and the outcome. If the outcome was that they didn't make it, say so and describe how he documented it and escalated to the manager. That shows judgment.

**3. "You built HubSpot KPI dashboards at Nuve. Which five numbers would you look at every Monday for a 10-SDR team here, and what would make you intervene?"** *(running a team on metrics)*
> **Coaching:** Name a funnel: dials, connects, conversations, meetings set, show rate, meeting-to-opp, pipeline sourced. Give thresholds ("connect rate under X for two weeks means a list or timing problem, not a rep problem"). Use his own benchmarks: 76% show rate, 85% meeting-to-opp, 85+ touches a day. Tie it to the weekly rep-metrics report he sent the Big Bus sales leader.

**4. "Salesforce is a must here. Tell me about the last Salesforce report you built for a sales leader. How would you build an SDR leaderboard and a meeting-to-opp conversion report in Sales Cloud?"** *(Salesforce depth)*
> **Coaching:** Lead with the CROP NPSP build: a 12-stage pipeline, custom objects, reports and dashboards tracking $2.8M. Then be candid that his recent daily CRM is HubSpot, and walk through the Sales Cloud equivalents (report types, Activities, Opportunities by Lead Source, dashboard filters). Do 2–3 hours of Trailhead first so the vocabulary is current.

**5. "What sequencing platform have you run, and how did you structure a cadence in it? Have you used Salesloft specifically?"** *(Salesloft / tech stack)*
> **Coaching:** Answer the Salesloft question with a straight yes or no. Then describe the Intaker Home Services cadence: steps, channels (phone, SMS, email, LinkedIn), timing, and how he A/B tested openers. Mention rolling out Nooks, ZoomInfo and Chili Piper at Nuve as proof he can adopt and roll out new tools quickly. If the answer is no, offer a 30-day ramp plan.

**6. "You've been at Intaker six months, and your last four roles averaged about a year each. Why should I believe you'll stay and build this team for 2+ years?"** *(tenure)*
> **Coaching:** Own the pattern in one sentence, then show progression: street rep, then lead rep, then enablement, then founding SDR with team-lead duties, then GTM owner. Explain that each move was toward leading SDRs, and that this is the role all of it pointed to. Say why ServiceTitan specifically (the trades, the market he's already building) is a long-term fit. Don't criticize Intaker or Nuve.

**7. "Your SDR manager at Nuve: what did you do that he didn't, and how would he describe your working relationship if I called him?"** *(reference-check and maturity probe)*
> **Coaching:** Use partnership language. "I brought a frontline view of which reports leadership needed, set up the HubSpot rep dashboards, flagged stack gaps and sourced candidates, and he leaned on me for that." Never say "I taught my manager." Expect us to call Branden, and line him up beforehand.

**8. "It's your first 90 days. You inherit eight SDRs, two are under 50% of plan, and leadership wants pipeline up 20% next quarter. What do you do?"** *(running a team on metrics, recruiting, prioritization)*
> **Coaching:** Days 1–30: listen, ride along on calls, set a metrics baseline, hold 1:1s with every rep. Days 31–60: performance plans for the two low performers, a team call-coaching cadence, and sharper ICP and messaging. Days 61–90: hiring pipeline, and decisions on the low performers. Use the Nuve result (+20% output, +60 demos and +$126K closed-won a month from enablement) and the CROP results (28% faster ramp, reply rates up from 1.8% to 5.2%) as proof he has moved team numbers before.
