- **Most of the structure agrees with what I found:** headline formula and length, a first-person About with a hook and a call to action, context plus results in each role, accurate dates, recruiter-only "Open to Work" for employed candidates, and a focused skills list.

- **Four claims I did not use** because they're retired or I couldn't verify them: skill assessments, "30% more recruiter messages" for verified profiles, "17x views from endorsements," and "keyword lists are penalized."
