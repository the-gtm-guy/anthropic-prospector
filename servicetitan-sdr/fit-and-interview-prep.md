## Risks and how to handle them

1. **"You've coached and led. Why an SDR role?"** This is the first question. Write your answer in your own words. A true version has three parts: you like the live conversation and the win, ServiceTitan's SDR program is a structured path into Account Executive with call-review coaching, and you'd rather earn the next role inside the system than walk in on a title. Leadership comes after you've proven it there. Don't call the job "a stepping stone" and don't say it's beneath what you've done.
2. **"Why leave Intaker after about six months?"** Expect it. I don't know your actual reason, so write one honest, positive sentence (a larger sales org, the trades market, a clear AE path). Don't criticize Intaker, Nuve or anyone there.
3. **Four employers in under four years** (Big Bus, CROP, Nuve, Intaker). Frame them as build-stage roles where you were brought in to build, and say you're looking for a place to grow for years.
4. **Gong.** Say it straight: "I haven't used Gong. I've recorded and reviewed my calls in Fireflies, and I picked up HubSpot, Nooks and ZoomInfo quickly." Don't claim it.
5. **Pay.** The posting says $28.85 an hour in California (about $60K a year base) plus variable commission and equity. That's well below a manager-level target. Decide before the call whether you're optimizing for the path. Ask about on-target earnings, average attainment and the usual time to promotion instead of negotiating the base.

## Likely questions

1. **Walk me through your Nuve numbers.** Lead with the demos (30-35 booked, 76% show rate), then the closes, then the 162%. Use the quota math above.
2. **Cold call me.** Open with who you are in one line, give a reason tied to their world (missed calls and booked jobs for a contractor), and ask for a short, specific next step. Practice it out loud twice with the HVAC/plumbing/electrical version.
3. **Tell me about feedback that changed how you sell.** Use a real one. Skeleton: the situation, what the feedback was, what you changed, the number that moved. This is the "coachable" answer, and it's the one you haven't written yet.
4. **How do you run your day?** Describe your block structure: calling windows, research, follow-ups, calendar. Only say what you actually do.
5. **How do you qualify?** MEDDPICC at Intaker: what you ask early to tell a real opportunity from a polite maybe. Say you use it as a framework (you aren't certified).
6. **Why trades? Why ServiceTitan?** Say what you learned building the Home Services playbook this year (only what's true), and that ServiceTitan is the system contractors run on.
7. **What tools have you used, and how fast do you learn new ones?** The Salesloft pilot, ZoomInfo and Nooks rollout and your Clay and n8n work are the proof. Then the honest Gong answer.
8. **Where do you want to be in two years?** Account Executive first, leadership later. That's "either" with an order, and it matches the JD's "set you up as an Account Executive."
9. **What would your last manager say about you?** Pick a strength and a growth area you've actually worked on.
10. **Comp expectations?** Say you're focused on the role and the path, and ask about the full commission plan before you give a number.
